MediaGo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026  •  App Version: 1.0.0  •  Developer: NKM IT Solution

MediaGo ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by NKM IT Solution (Pro. Naresh Meghwal), is an on-device media downloader and offline audio/video player. Your privacy is fundamental to how MediaGo is built: the App works directly on your device and does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data on external developer servers.

1 Information We Do Not Collect

MediaGo is designed with a local-first architecture. We do not:

  • Require account registration, phone numbers, or personal profiles to use the App.
  • Collect, upload, or analyze your downloaded files, local music/video library, or playback history.
  • Sell, rent, or trade any user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Track your physical GPS location or access your contacts, SMS messages, or call logs.

2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To deliver core features smoothly, MediaGo stores small operational records exclusively on your device's private app storage:

  • Download History & Deleted Blacklist: Titles, download statuses, local file paths/URIs, and a list of items you have deleted so removed files are not re-imported during storage refreshes.
  • Playlists & Player Preferences: Custom playlists, favorite tracks, equalizer band presets, and playback settings.
  • Optional Session Cookies: If you voluntarily choose to sign in via the optional Instagram connection inside the navigation drawer, session cookies are stored only on your device to fulfill download requests you initiate. You can disconnect and wipe these cookies at any time.
  • On-Device Clipboard Check: When MediaGo is brought to the foreground, it checks your clipboard locally to show a quick "Smart Paste & Parse" button if a supported media link is detected. Clipboard contents are never stored or transmitted unless you explicitly choose to parse the link.

3 Android Permissions & How They Are Used

MediaGo only requests permissions required for downloading, saving, playing, and visualizing your media:

INTERNET
Fetches media metadata and downloads user-requested videos, reels, audio, or images directly from the link you provide.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / STORAGE
Saves downloaded media to your device's Downloads/MediaGo folder and allows the built-in player to browse, play, rename, and delete your local audio and video files.
RECORD_AUDIO & MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS
Used strictly by Android's audio visualizer and 8-preset equalizer during local playback to render real-time audio waveforms and bass/treble effects. MediaGo never records, saves, or transmits microphone or voice audio.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Displays active download progress, completion alerts, and lock-screen media playback controls.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & WAKE_LOCK
Keeps ongoing downloads and background audio/video playback running reliably when the screen turns off or you switch apps.

4 Third-Party Platforms & External Links

When you paste or share a link from external websites or social platforms (such as YouTube, Instagram, X/Twitter, Facebook, or direct media URLs), MediaGo sends network requests directly from your device to those platforms to resolve and download the requested media.

Those external services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service. We do not control how third-party websites handle requests made from your device.

User Responsibility: Please ensure you have the necessary rights or permission from the content owner before downloading, saving, or sharing any media, and always comply with applicable copyright laws.

5 Data Control & Deletion

Because all MediaGo data resides locally on your device, you remain in full control at all times:

  • Delete Individual Files: Tap the delete/trash icon on any downloaded item to permanently remove both the physical media file and its history entry from MediaGo.
  • Clear Optional Session Cookies: Open the navigation drawer and tap Instagram Connected → Disconnect to immediately erase locally saved cookies.
  • Erase All App Data: Clearing MediaGo's app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App permanently removes all preferences, playlists, and history.

6 Children's Privacy

MediaGo does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). Because the App does not collect personal user accounts or transmit telemetry, no personal data of minors is collected by us.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to MediaGo or changes in legal and platform requirements. Any updates will be reflected by the "Effective Date" shown at the top of this page and inside the App.
